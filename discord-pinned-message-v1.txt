**PULSE — v1.0** · Deal logger & leaderboard
---

**What it is**
Pulse tracks **fiber deals you close** — no points, **rank = total deals**. Everything saves to the team’s leaderboard file on the bot host.

**How to use it (3 rules)**
• **Log in the right channel** — team / “blitz” name comes from the **channel name**, so log where your squad actually posts.
• **Quick log (no slash):** type **`1g`**, **`1gig`**, **`500`**, or **`2x 1g`** — Pulse logs it automatically.
• **Leaderboards (no slash):** type **`lb`**, **`daily`**, **`weekly`**, **`master`**, **`mydeals`**, **`quarter`**, **`markets`** in chat.
• **Wrong log?** **`/remove-last`** — only removes **your** most recent log.

**Admins only**
**/export** — Download all logs as CSV‑style text.
**/reset-weekly** — Archives this week’s totals, then **starts a fresh weekly board** (all‑time history stays).

**Tips**
• Type **`/`** in Discord anytime — every command shows a short built‑in description.
• If the bot is **offline**, logs won’t save until it’s back on — that’s normal for a bot that runs on a PC unless it’s hosted 24/7.
