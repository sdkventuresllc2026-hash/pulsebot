## 🎯 What we do

We sell fiber internet door to door. We're carrier-agnostic — right now that's **T-Mobile Fiber, Kinetic, and Brightspeed**, and that list will keep changing. What doesn't change: the doors, the reps, and this server.

## 💰 How you get paid

**This is a 100% commission job.** Nobody hands you a paycheck for showing up. Your effort is your income — that's the deal, and it's why the ceiling is where it is.

Three things to burn into your brain:

**1. You get paid on the INSTALL, not the sale.**
A signed order is not money. An installed customer is money. A sale that never installs pays you nothing.

**2. That means the job isn't over when you leave the door.**
Text your customer the day before their install. Confirm they'll be home. Confirm they know the window. The reps at the top of the leaderboard are not lucky — they confirm every single install.

**3. Cancels are recoverable.**
A cancel is not a dead deal until you've actually called. Customers reschedule constantly. Chase it.

Your rate depends on your agreement and your speed tier. Payday lands on a set schedule per carrier — it's posted in **#announcements** every cycle. Ask in **#ask-anything** if you're unsure what you're owed.

## 📊 Logging your deals — this is important

**Pulse** is our bot. Every deal you close gets logged in your market channel, and it takes two seconds.

Just type the speed. That's the whole thing:

```
1g          →  one 1-Gig deal
2g          →  one 2-Gig deal
2x 1g       →  two 1-Gig deals
1g 2g       →  one of each
```

Pulse confirms it instantly and updates the leaderboard. **If it isn't logged, it didn't happen** — the leaderboard, callouts, and competitions all run off this.

📸 **Upload your order confirmation screenshot with it.** Same message. That's how we track the customer through to install so nothing falls through the cracks.

**Useful commands:**
| | |
|---|---|
| `/mydeals` | your numbers |
| `/leaderboard` | your market's standings |
| `/master` | every market, head to head |
| `/daily` | today across the company |
| `/remove-last` | logged something wrong? undo it |

Full command list lives in **#pulse-help**.

## ⚡ The standard

We're a small team doing serious numbers. A few things we hold everybody to:

- **Log every deal, same day.** No exceptions.
- **Confirm every install the day before.** This is the difference between a good month and a great one.
- **Answer your customers.** Even the cancels. Especially the cancels.
- **Pick people up.** Somebody has a rough day, you're the reason they knock the next one.

Nobody here is competing against you. The leaderboard is a mirror, not a war.
